TKM · Privacy

What this site remembers about you.

Short version: very little, nothing that can be sold, and you can opt out.

What is collected

  • A random visitor ID created in your browser — not linked to your identity.
  • Your answers on the welcome screen: the name you typed (optional), why you're visiting, and your sound, motion and starting-section choices.
  • How the visit went: which sections you reached, which links you used (e.g. the brief form, résumé, socials), time on page and how far you scrolled.
  • Coarse context: device type (phone / tablet / desktop), browser language, country, and the website that linked you here (domain only).

What is never collected

  • Your IP address is not stored. Country comes from the hosting provider's network header.
  • No advertising or third-party tracking cookies, no fingerprinting, no selling or sharing of data.

Why

To personalise your visit, remember your preferences next time, and help Tarun understand which work people find useful.

How long

Data is deleted automatically after 12 months of inactivity.

Your choices

  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded.
  • You can clear what this browser remembers below. To have stored data deleted, contact Tarun via LinkedIn or Instagram.
